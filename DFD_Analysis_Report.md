# Data Flow Diagram (DFD) Analysis Report
## Sector 13 Welfare Society - Digital Management System

### Project Analysis Summary

Based on the comprehensive analysis of your Sector 13 Welfare Society project, I have identified the following key components:

#### **External Entities (Users):**
1. **Admin/SuperAdmin** - System administrators with full access
2. **President** - Executive leadership role
3. **Secretary** - Administrative management role  
4. **Manager** - Operational management role
5. **Member** - Regular society members
6. **Employee** - Society employees
7. **Donor** - External donors making contributions
8. **Visitor** - Public visitors to the website

#### **Main Processes Identified:**
1. **Authentication & Login Management** (1.0)
2. **User Management** (2.0)
3. **Member Management** (3.0)
4. **Donation Management** (4.0)
5. **Notice Management** (5.0)
6. **Employee Management** (6.0)
7. **Attendance Management** (7.0)
8. **Leave Management** (8.0)
9. **Content Management** (9.0)
10. **Report Generation** (10.0)

#### **Data Stores:**
1. **User Database** - Stores user accounts and authentication data
2. **Member Database** - Stores member information and profiles
3. **Donation Database** - Stores donation records and payment information
4. **Employee Database** - Stores employee information and HR data
5. **Attendance Database** - Stores attendance and leave records
6. **Notice Database** - Stores notices and announcements
7. **Content Database** - Stores website content and organizational information

#### **Key Data Flows:**
- User authentication and authorization
- Member registration and profile management
- Donation processing and tracking
- Employee attendance and leave management
- Notice creation and distribution
- Report generation and data retrieval
